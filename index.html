From sunlight to your outlets

Four things happen between your roof and the lamp on your desk.

  1. 01

    Sunlight

    Your roof collects light every day of the year, even through cloud cover.

  2. 02

    Solar panels

    Panels convert that sunlight into direct current (DC) electricity.

  3. 03

    Inverter

    The inverter turns DC into the alternating current (AC) your home runs on.

  4. 04

    Battery storage

    Whatever you don't use gets stored and drawn down after the sun sets.
